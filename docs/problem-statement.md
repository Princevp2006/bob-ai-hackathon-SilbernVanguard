# Problem Statement

## The Audience
Grid Operators, Utility Managers, and Field Maintenance Crews.

## The Problem
Unplanned power grid outages and catastrophic transformer failures cause massive economic disruptions and safety hazards. Currently, grid operators struggle to proactively predict when a transformer will fail due to internal chemical degradation (like corrosive sulfur/DBDS buildup) or when severe weather patterns will trigger localized outages.

## Why Existing Solutions Fail
Current SCADA systems rely on threshold-based alerts (e.g., "Alert if temperature > 90C"). This is purely reactive. By the time a threshold is breached, physical damage to the grid infrastructure may have already begun. Furthermore, analyzing 14+ dissolved chemical gases in transformer oil is computationally complex for a human to do in real-time.

## The Pain
This forces operators into a **reactive** (fixing things after they break) rather than **preventive** maintenance approach. Unplanned outages result in millions of dollars in regulatory fines, lost industrial revenue, and unhappy consumers.
